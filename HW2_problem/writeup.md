Hosting at a Restaurant:
This homework question is meant to mirror the thought process of a host at a restaurant and the decisions they must make when seating tables on a busy night. Use the following guidelines to create code to help make decisions based on certain conditions. 

Inputs:
How many people are in the party?
Has the entire party arrived?
Do they have a reservation
Do they want a high top or a low table
Did they request a booth 
Conditions:
If there are more than 4 people and they don’t have a reservation, it will be a 30 minute wait.
If they have a reservation they will be seated immediately 
If it’s a walk-in of 4 or less people and they are willing to sit at a high top they can be seated immediately
If there’s a walk in of 4 or less that will only sit in a booth, it will be an hour wait
If there’s a walk in of 4 or less that prefers a regular table, it will be a 20 minute wait.
